Iron Will

We don't get to choose who we are born to be,
Nor do we choose the world we are left with...
I know one thing for certain though.
Giving in and giving up gurantees failure,
But fighting gives us a chance at success.

‎

One of the first robots to be created for DLZ Robotics, Iron Will was designed to stand for the unbreakable will of the people that choose to fight. Though the first publicly released robot was Bulwark in 2023, Iron Will's existance dates back to 2019, only taking a true 3D form in 2024.

The design of Iron Will boasts the origingal design philosophy of Zuckerberg Corp. before it went public and became DLZ Robotics. Rather than taking a distorted humanoid form, Iron Will and some of the original robots took more heroic and warrior-like builds as the bots were designed to be defenders against anything that threatened the integrity of humanity.
