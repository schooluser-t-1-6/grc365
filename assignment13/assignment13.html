DLZ Robotics

A random concept from 2019, DLZ Robotics was originally known as "Zuckerberg Corp.", an organization that created experimental robots to protect the world from any possible unforeseen threats. In 2023, the concept was rebranded as "DLZ Robotics" and evolved to become a pack of playable bosses in Garry's Mod.

In the modern iteration, Dr. Lloyd Zuckerberg is a solo creator who was once friends with another creator, someone who was martyred over a system that runs on hypocrisy. DLZ developed experimental robots to act as guardians that protected himself and the innocent, eventually enhancing some of the bots using an ethereal material he develops: liquid horror. Though liquid horror has its limits, its biggest strength is the power to bypass invincibility, forcing the many god-like oppressors either adapt skill or die a pushover.
